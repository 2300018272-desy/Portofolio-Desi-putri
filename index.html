01 Profile

Curious by nature.
Thoughtful by design.

I'm Desi Putri, a seventh-semester student at Ahmad Dahlan University, designer, and developer who believes good design should be simple, useful, and a little unexpected.

My work sits at the intersection of visual storytelling and functional interfaces. I enjoy taking an idea from a rough sketch to a polished experience that people can actually use and enjoy.

My process starts with understanding people, then turning those insights into thoughtful flows, wireframes, prototypes, and responsive interfaces that work beautifully across devices. I'm currently balancing my studies with hands-on projects that help me grow as a designer and developer.
